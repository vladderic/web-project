Project 5.0

by Alex

After three years of saving and a lot of YouTube rabbit holes, my first build is finally on the road. Dropped in a new 5.0 V8, upgraded the suspension with coilovers, and resprayed it 'Violent Violet'. It's not the fastest, but it's mine.

The Sleeper Civic

by Jordan

Everyone thinks it's a beat-up daily driver. Under the hood it's a K-swap with a big turbo — 400 horses in a car nobody looks twice at. My favorite part is the look on people's faces at the lights.

  • Sam: A true sleeper. Love it.

Desert Fox

by Chris

Built for overlanding trips with my dad. Long-travel suspension, skid plates, and a roof tent. It's hauled us across three states and never let us down. Comfort in the dirt is underrated.

  • Priya: The roof tent is the dream.

Apex M3

by Lena

A track-day toy I bought as a rusted shell. Rebuilt the S54 engine, added carbon bucket seats and a half-cage. Ran my first track event last month and I'm hooked. Brakes and tires are the real heroes here.

  • Raj: Track days are addictive!

Neon Dream

by Tessa

Budget build done right. Coilovers, sticky tires, and a clean wrap with a retro 90s vibe. No crazy power, just pure handling. Corners feel like a go-kart. Best $4,000 I've ever spent.

  • Omar: That wrap is so clean.
  • Kit: Go-kart handling is the best kind.
